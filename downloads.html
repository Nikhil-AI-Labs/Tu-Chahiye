A word on how these were made

so you know what you are downloading

Every PDF here is rendered from the page you can see on this site, through assets/print.css, which is a real print layout rather than a screenshot: the dark sheet becomes paper, the navigation goes, the halftone comes off, the spot inks stay, and nothing that belongs together is split across a page break.

You can make your own at any time. Press Ctrl+P (⌘+P on a Mac) on any page and choose Save as PDF — you will get the same thing, current as of that moment. On a phone it is under Share → Print. Every solution opens itself before the dialog appears.

If a page has been edited since these were built, run python tools/make-pdfs.py and they are all rebuilt in about a minute.
