How to use it

three weeks, or three days
1 · Read the notes

Each subject is written from zero in plain English — every formula is explained the first time it appears, and every worked example from your own tutorials is solved step by step. Start at chapter one and do not skip the examples.

2 · Sit the quiz

Only once, so do it after a proper read rather than to see the questions. The answer key afterwards tells you which chapter every mistake came from, which is the fastest revision list you will get.

3 · Work the papers

Do the past papers with the solution covered, then the predicted paper against a clock. The pattern page tells you which topics have appeared every single year — those are not optional.
