House rules

  1. One attempt per name. Once your name is on the board it can never sit the quiz again, so pick the name you want your friends to see.

  2. The clock does not stop. It starts when you press START and at 45:00 whatever you have is submitted for you.

  3. Jump between questions and change your mind as often as you like — nothing is locked until you submit.

  4. Close the tab by accident and you have not lost it. Reopen the link and you land on the same question with the time that was left.

  5. A tie goes to whoever finished faster. Think, but do not sit there admiring the question.
